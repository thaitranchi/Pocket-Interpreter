## Full description

Pocket Interpreter is a fully offline, real-time voice translator for English and Vietnamese (EN-VI). No internet connection required — every translation happens on your device.

CONVERSATION MADE EASY
• Push-to-talk: hold the button, speak, release to translate
• Continuous mode: hands-free live interpretation during conversations
• Live subtitles as you speak, with spoken translation playback

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Translations run on-device: no audio or transcript ever leaves your device
• No account, no tracking
• Microphone is used only while you are actively translating

FREE EVERY DAY, GO PRO ONCE
• Free: unlimited text translation + 5 minutes of voice interpretation per day, with a small banner ad
• Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): unlimited voice interpretation, no ads, priority processing, and advanced features
• Pay once, own it forever — no monthly fees

BUILT FOR TRAVEL AND DAILY USE
• Perfect for travel, work meetings, or talking with family and friends
• Works without Wi-Fi or mobile data
• Download the offline language pack once and use it anywhere

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick your source and target language (English or Vietnamese)
2. Choose push-to-talk or continuous mode
3. Speak, and get instant translated text and voice — all on-device

Pocket Interpreter is designed for privacy-first, reliable conversation. Your words stay on your phone, always.

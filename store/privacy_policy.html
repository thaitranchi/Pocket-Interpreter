Privacy Policy — Pocket Interpreter

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Pocket Interpreter ("the app") is developed and maintained by Trần Chí Thái. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information. The app is designed to be fully private and offline by default.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any translation content or audio. The app does not require an account and does not use analytics.

Advertising

The free version of the app displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob uses your device's advertising identifier (advertising ID). This identifier is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. The Pro version (one-time purchase) removes all ads, and no advertising data is collected while ads are disabled.

Microphone Permission

The app requests access to your device's microphone for the sole purpose of speech recognition while you actively use the translation features. Audio is processed in real time on your device and is not recorded, saved, or sent to any server. This permission is used only while the app is in use.

Data Processing

All translation and speech processing happens locally on your device. Your speech and text are never uploaded, and no internet connection is required for the translation features to function.

Offline Language Packs

The app may download language model files to enable offline translation. These files are standard model data and do not contain any of your personal information.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party trackers or analytics. Google AdMob is the only third-party service, used solely to display banner ads in the free version.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Advertising identifiers used by AdMob are generally not considered personal information under applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

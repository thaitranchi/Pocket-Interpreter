Privacy Policy — Pocket Interpreter

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Pocket Interpreter ("the app") is developed and maintained by Trần Chí Thái. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information. The app is designed to be fully private and offline by default.

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not use analytics, and does not show advertisements.

Microphone Permission

The app requests access to your device's microphone for the sole purpose of speech recognition while you actively use the translation features. Audio is processed in real time on your device and is not recorded, saved, or sent to any server. This permission is used only while the app is in use.

Data Processing

All translation and speech processing happens locally on your device. Your speech and text are never uploaded, and there is no internet connection required for the app to function.

Offline Language Packs

The app may download language model files to enable offline translation. These files are standard model data and do not contain any of your personal information.

Third-Party Services

The app contains no third-party services, trackers, or advertising SDKs.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
